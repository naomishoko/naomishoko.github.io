Nomvelo Shoko

Business analytics graduate student bringing together experience in financial administration, business operations and data-informed decision-making.

My Story

I am pursuing a Master of Science in Business Analytics at Brandeis International Business School in Massachusetts. My professional background spans business administration, financial reporting and operational support, and I am building on that experience with statistical analysis, programming, data visualization and business modeling.

Before graduate school, I worked in financial administration and business support roles across different organizations. These experiences helped me understand how important reliable information, well-organized processes and clear reporting are to everyday business decisions.

My studies have given me opportunities to examine economic trends, analyze university mobility patterns, explore customer data and model business systems. I enjoy connecting analytical methods with practical questions: What is changing? Why might it be happening? What should an organization consider doing next?

What I Want to Do

I am interested in business analyst, data analyst and business analytics consulting opportunities, particularly where I can help organizations and entrepreneurs translate data into actionable insights. I value careful analysis, clear communication and recommendations that decision-makers can understand and use.

Beyond the Numbers

My foundation in psychology and business administration shapes how I approach analytics. I believe useful analysis considers not only the data, but also the people, processes and decisions behind it.
