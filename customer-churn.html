Customer Churn: Decision & Validation Design

Designing a leakage-aware, capacity-constrained approach to prioritizing next-month customer retention outreach using a synthetic customer-month dataset.

PythonpandasData qualityData leakageBaseline evaluation

Business decision

A customer retention manager must decide, at the end of month t, which existing customers to prioritize for potential outreach about churn in month t+1. The exercise limits selections to the top 10% of customer-month records. The unit of analysis is one customer in one month; a customer can appear repeatedly.

Decision boundary: Identifying higher-risk records is not evidence that contacting those customers would prevent churn.

The course-provided synthetic file contains 14 columns, with no duplicate customer-month pairs. Monthly coverage falls from 1,200 records in month 1 to 532 in month 12. The reason for missing customer-month observations is not documented, so absence cannot automatically be treated as churn.

Feature availability & leakage

Candidate scoring-time inputs included tenure, contract type, plan level, current-month usage, support tickets and a documented payment-delay flag. Usage change requires further definition before inclusion.

Excluded from scoring

FieldReason
next_month_usageContains future month t+1 information.
final_invoice_amountPost-outcome billing field; even its missingness reveals the recorded outcome in this file.
payment_failedEvent timing and scoring-time availability are undocumented.
account_closedLater operational outcome, not a scoring-time predictor.

In this historical file, final_invoice_amount is present for all 689 recorded churn rows and missing for all 9,278 non-churn rows. Using this pattern would create severe target leakage.

Fixed baseline: support-ticket ranking

The baseline prioritizes records with more current-month support tickets. It sorts by support-ticket count descending, then month ascending and customer ID ascending to break ties, and selects exactly 996 of 9,967 rows.

Validation plan & next question

The proposed next step is a controlled temporal comparison using months 1–9 versus months 10–12, checking whether next-month labels are actually available before training. The same ranking rule, tie-breaks, contact capacity and metrics should be applied consistently.

This would assess later-month performance for existing customers; it would not by itself establish generalization to unseen customers or whether retention offers reduce churn.

My contribution & reproducibility

I prepared the decision and data contract, investigated dataset coverage and missingness, identified leakage risks, calculated descriptive churn rates, implemented the fixed ranking baseline in Python/pandas, and documented its evaluation limitations.

The assignment package includes a decision memo, an evidence appendix and a reproducible Python script (hw1_analysis.py). The underlying course-provided CSV is not included in the submitted ZIP, so the script requires access to that dataset to rerun.
